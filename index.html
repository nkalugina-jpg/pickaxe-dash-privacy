Pickaxe Dash Privacy Policy

Android app · Whaler Games · Last updated November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Pickaxe Dash, previously tested as Oreplunge, uses information when you play. For privacy questions or requests, contact m24450810@gmail.com.

Game saves and settings

Pickaxe Dash stores your progress, game currency, settings, and playtime locally on your device so you can resume playing. The game does not require a player account. You can delete local data by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings.

Analytics and diagnostics

The app uses native SayKit to send game events to SayAnalytics. Events include app loading, chapter attempts and results, tutorial progress, changes to virtual currency, and offers to watch rewarded ads. We use these events to understand how the game performs and improve it. The SDK and its service providers may process device or app identifiers, IP address, device and app information, and diagnostic data to operate and troubleshoot their services.

Advertising

Pickaxe Dash includes optional rewarded ads and ads between runs. SayKit and its advertising partners may process advertising or device identifiers, IP address, device information, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and detect fraud. Personalized advertising depends on your region, device settings, and consent where required. You can manage your advertising ID in Android Settings and use the consent options presented by the app where available. See SayGames’ privacy policy for information about its providers and privacy choices.

Reviews and support

The game can ask Google Play to show a rating prompt. Google handles the rating or review you choose to submit. If you email us, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond.

Retention and your rights

Game saves remain on your device until you clear them. Information processed online is kept as needed for the purposes described above, including to comply with legal requirements and resolve disputes. You can email us to request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights that apply where you live. Include that your request concerns Pickaxe Dash. Device data deletion and opt-out requests may require information that helps us locate the relevant records.
